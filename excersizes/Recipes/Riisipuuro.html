In Finland riisipuuro is traditionally served with a jam made of prunes. Typically you’d buy prunes and gently stew them, serving it as a hot topping. It can also be served with other traditional desserts like kissel, which is a kind of fruit sauce that is thickened with potato starch. Again, naturally gluten free. If you don’t want to make a sauce, it’s also great with brown sugar, cinnamon and butter on top. Or you could just eat it as is. - Kati Keksi
